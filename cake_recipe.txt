"Ingredients: - 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour - 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar - 3/4 cup unsalted butter (room temperature) - 2 large eggs - 1 cup whole milk - 1 tbsp vanilla extract - 1 tsp baking powder - 1/2 tsp salt Instructions: 1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease two 9-inch round pans. 2. In a bowl, whisk flour, baking powder, and salt. 3. In another bowl, cream butter and sugar until fluffy. Add eggs one at a time. 4. Gradually add dry ingredients to wet mixture, alternating with milk. 5. Fold in vanilla extract. Divide batter between pans. 6. Bake 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserts cleanly. 7. Let cool completely before frosting."
